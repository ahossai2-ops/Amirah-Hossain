I'm a sophomore Finance & Marketing student at Eastern Michigan University with a passion for the intersection of brand identity and human connection. Growing up across Bangladesh and Michigan gave me a global perspective — I've learned that great marketing isn't about selling, it's about understanding people.

From managing multi-platform social campaigns at EMU's Rec/IM to serving as a campus ambassador in the Admissions office, I've built real experience in content strategy, audience engagement, and data-informed storytelling. I'm driven by brands that mean something.

Outside of work I geek out over brand case studies, consumer psychology, and finding the perfect hook. I'm fluent in English and Bengali, conversational in Hindi and Urdu — and I believe speaking more languages means understanding more markets.
